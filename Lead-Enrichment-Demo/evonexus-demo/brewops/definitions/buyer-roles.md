# Definition: Buyer Roles

<!-- inject-when: a contact-discovery worker is classifying the humans found at a shop, or generating outreach targeting -->

A single coffee shop has several humans, and **they are not interchangeable.** Selling to the wrong one wastes the cycle. Each contact gets exactly one role label.

| Role | Who, at a coffee shop | What they control | How BrewOps treats them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decision-maker** | Owner / co-owner | Signs the contract, owns the budget | The yes. But often reached *through* a champion, not cold. |
| **Champion** | Café manager / GM | Lives the operational pain daily, advocates internally | **Primary entry point.** The person who feels the scheduling/inventory mess. |
| **Gatekeeper** | Head barista / shift lead | Controls access, screens vendors | Route around respectfully; never the pitch target. |
| **Influencer** | Bookkeeper / accountant / a trusted regular consultant | Shapes the decision without owning it | Arm with ROI material; cite in the angle. |
| **End-user** | Baristas, shift staff | Use the tool daily; can kill adoption | Their experience is an *objection source* and an *adoption driver*, not a sales target. |

## The operational-buyer principle (the load-bearing rule)

> **Sell to the person who owns the pain, not the person with the fanciest title.**

At a coffee shop the pain of bad scheduling and stockouts is felt by the **manager** (champion), not the owner who may be hands-off, and definitely not the barista. So contact-discovery **prioritizes operational titles over ownership titles**:

1. Café Manager / General Manager *(champion, start here)*
2. Operations Lead / Multi-unit Manager
3. Owner / Co-owner *(decision-maker, escalation, not entry)*
4. Head Barista / Shift Lead *(gatekeeper, route around)*

This exactly mirrors Novara's real rule: target the **practice manager**, not the **physician**. Same principle, different domain.

## Quality bar for creating a contact

Never write a contact without a **verified LinkedIn or email**. A name with no findable footprint is not worth adding, it pollutes the CRM and can't be actioned.

> Bridge: role is a **classification label** the model assigns from the title, and it changes downstream routing. "Champion" vs "gatekeeper" is the difference between a warm path and a wasted touch.
